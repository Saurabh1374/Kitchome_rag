# Natural Stone Countertop Maintenance & Stain Removal

## Daily Sanitization & Cleaning
1. Wipe granite and marble surfaces using warm water, mild dish soap, and a microfiber cloth.
2. Avoid acidic cleaners such as vinegar, lemon juice, or bleach on natural stone, as acid dulls and etches protective sealant.

## Sealing & Waterproofing
Reseal natural granite and marble countertops every 12 to 18 months using a penetrating water-based sealer to prevent wine, oil, and coffee stains.
