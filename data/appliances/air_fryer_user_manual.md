# Air Fryer Pro 5000 User Manual & Troubleshooting Guide

## Specifications & Electrical Requirements
- Model: AF-PRO-5000
- Voltage: 120V AC, 60Hz
- Power Consumption: 1500 Watts
- Capacity: 5.8 Quarts

## Error Codes & Troubleshooting
- **Error E1**: Open circuit sensor error. Unplug unit for 10 minutes and retry. If issue persists, contact service.
- **Error E2**: Short circuit thermal cutoff error. Ensure heating element ventilation is not blocked.
- **Error E3**: Overheating protection activated. Disconnect power immediately and allow heating element to cool down completely.

## Maintenance & Filter Replacement
Clean the removable heating element filter every 30 uses. Use non-abrasive soap and warm water only. Do not immerse main unit motor in water.
