# Crispy Air Fryer Chicken Wings Recipe

## Ingredients
- 2 lbs chicken wings, split into flats and drumettes
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp salt and black pepper
- Baking powder (secret for crispiness!)

## Cooking Instructions
1. Preheat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C) for 3 minutes.
2. Pat the chicken wings dry with paper towels.
3. Toss wings with olive oil, seasonings, and 1 tsp baking powder.
4. Arrange wings in a single layer in the air fryer basket without overcrowding.
5. Cook for 20 minutes, flipping halfway through. Serve hot with garlic butter sauce.
